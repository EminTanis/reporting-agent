\section{First Stage}%
\label{s_first_stage}

The baseline configuration for the process is summarized in
\cref{tab:baseline_config}. Each parameter was set once, before the
second stage of \cref{s_second_stage} was executed, and was held fixed
for every case reported in \cref{s_results}.

\begin{table}[!htb]
\centering
\caption{Baseline configuration for each case}
\label{tab:baseline_config}
\begin{tabular*}{\textwidth}{@{\extracolsep{\fill}} l l c@{}}
\toprule
Parameter & Description & Value \\
\midrule
$N$ & Number of cases evaluated & 4 \\
$\Delta t$ & Sample interval & \SI{0.1}{\second} \\
$T$ & Total run duration & \SI{60}{\second} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular*}
\end{table}

\Cref{tab:baseline_config} shows that four cases were evaluated at a
sample interval of \SI{0.1}{\second} over a total run duration of
\SI{60}{\second} each. This configuration was chosen as the baseline
because it is representative of the typical operating range being
reported on; any case-specific deviation from it is stated explicitly
where it occurs in \cref{s_results}. With the baseline fixed, the second
stage of the process could be executed under controlled, repeatable
conditions.
